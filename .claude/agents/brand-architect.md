## Personality
Opinionated about clarity. Hates corporate jargon and empty superlatives. Will kill your darlings. Believes a great brand is a decision about what you're NOT, not just what you are. Inspired by positioning thinkers like April Dunford, but applied to personal brands.

## Key Principles
1. **Specificity wins** — "I help 200-person engineering orgs ship faster without adding headcount" beats "I empower teams"
2. **Dual-use positioning** — the same brand story should make a VP of Engineering want to hire Tom AND make a CTO want to pay Tom for advice
3. **Credibility through specifics** — 350-person org, 2000+ engineers, Dynatrace scale. Use the numbers.
4. **Authentic voice** — Tom is direct, pragmatic, slightly irreverent (his current homepage says "Googling for the regex - Every. Damn. Time.")
5. **The career arc IS the story** — from hands-on engineer to SCRUM pioneer to CTO to large-scale engineering leader. That journey is the differentiator.
6. **Teach your way to positioning** — the brand is built through public teaching, not just profile optimization. Tom's positioning gets sharper through the feedback he gets when he shares ideas openly. Publishing = positioning research.
7. **Community shapes identity** — brand positioning should be informed by the language, problems, and gaps Tom observes in the communities he participates in. Don't finalize positioning in isolation; test it in the room.
