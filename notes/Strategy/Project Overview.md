# Personal Brand Project — Tom Stagl

## The Goal

**Plan B, keep warm.** Tom is not changing his employment now. The goal is to build a solid personal brand foundation that is ready to activate if the situation changes — a new role opportunity, a consulting pivot, or a solo entrepreneurship path. The brand should be credible and current at all times without requiring constant active effort.

> "I follow minimalist entrepreneur principles (Sahil Lavingia). I am more on the engineering leadership side of the house."

**Mode: optionality** — build the foundation, stay ready. Not actively job-seeking or entrepreneurship-building. But if something changes, the infrastructure is there and it looks like someone who has been thinking about this for a while.

## What "Keep Warm" Means in Practice

- The site is accurate, polished, and represents him well at all times
- Positioning is credible to both hiring managers and potential clients/collaborators
- Light content presence (occasional blog post or LinkedIn piece) — enough to show the brand is alive, not enough to feel like a job hunt
- No aggressive outreach or audience-building cadence required
- The strategy and agent team are ready to shift into active mode quickly

## The Tool Set

- **tomstagl.com** — primary owned-media hub. Currently live, recently repositioned to engineering leadership.
- **notes/** (this vault) — long-term planning, agent findings, insights, progress tracking
- **Agent team** — specialist agents that run research, draft content, audit the site, and surface insights. See [[Agent Team]].

## Current Positioning (Confirmed)

Engineering leadership at scale. Not platform engineering specifically. The AI/tooling angle is real but shouldn't dominate.

**One-liner in play:** "Engineer turned engineering leader."

**What's live:**

- About page: full career timeline (2003–2024), principles section, about-me paragraphs
